## R2 - Three engines and my choice
1: Godot- Mainly supports C# & GDScript. Is a scene editor. Godot game is a tree of scenes & each scene is a tree of nodes. Nodes are fundamental building blocks of your game. Popular games are the following: Brotato & Buckshot Roulette. Supports 2D&3D games. 
2: Unity- has the tools to create props, scenery and characters called gameobjects. Scripting in Unity is programing. Popular games are the following: Among us, pokemon go, & hollow knight.
3: Stride- Simliar to Unity but move on with game development with C# and more of 2D engine with some 3D. Popular games are the following: childern of the galaxy, distant worlds 2, & FPS infinite.
